# Health

Entry point for health. Three living notes cover most of it:

- `fitness.md` movement, diet, routine
- `therapy.md` mind, therapy, what helps
- [[medical-history]] records: timeline and what holds long term

Create the first two when you have something to put in them.

A new medical report **extends the existing note on the same diagnosis or body part**. A new note only for a new topic. The hub's timeline always gets a new row.
