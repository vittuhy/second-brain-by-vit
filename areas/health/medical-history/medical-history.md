# Medical history

The index of your medical records. Every topic has its own thorough extraction from the reports, this note holds only the timeline and what applies long term. The originals (PDFs, scans, images) are **not** in the repository, note where they are kept in the `source` field of each extraction.

## What applies long term

Allergies, ongoing conditions, implants that matter before imaging, operations, family history, blood type. Only what a new doctor would want first.

## How to add a report

1. Give Claude the report. It writes an extraction per the rules in CLAUDE.md: every value, every date, exact wording of conclusions, what the report does not say.
2. If the diagnosis or body part already has a note, the extraction **extends** it.
3. Add a row to the timeline above.
