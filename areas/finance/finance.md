# Finance

Entry point for everything about money. Start here, follow the links, search only if that fails.

## Suggested notes

Create them when you have the content, not before:

- `overview.md` accounts, income, fixed costs, the monthly picture (no account numbers, see CLAUDE.md)
- `taxes.md` what you file, when, who helps, where the originals are
- `insurance.md` one section per policy: what it covers, **what it excludes**, renewal date
- one note per loan or mortgage

## Documents

When you hand Claude a contract, policy or tax return, it writes a thorough extraction instead of a summary. The rules are in CLAUDE.md, section Documents and attachments.
