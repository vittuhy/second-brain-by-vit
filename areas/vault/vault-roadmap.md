# Vault roadmap

What is still worth building. **Every item has a trigger, not a date.** Until the trigger happens, nothing gets built.

## By trigger

- [ ] **A `MAP.md` in every top-level folder**, once you stop knowing what is in `notes/` or `areas/`. Roughly above 500 files.
- [ ] **Another collection in `collections/`**, once you have more than ten items of one kind loose in `notes/`.
- [ ] **Gmail and Drive connectors**, once there is a concrete repeated task for them. Not earlier, or it is a toy.
- [ ] **Your own automation**, once the same manual operation annoys you for the third time.

## Habits that have no file

- [ ] **Weekly review as a recurring calendar event**, Friday, fifteen minutes. Without an invitation it relies on remembering.
- [ ] **Read [[goals]] once a month** and edit it.
