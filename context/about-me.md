# About me

> Claude reads this before it advises you on anything personal. Write it in your own words, keep it short, keep it true. `/setup` fills it in with you; `/context-sprint` deepens it later.

## Who I am

Name, year of birth, where you live, languages.

## Work

What you do, for whom, and what a good week looks like.

## Household

Who lives with you, who depends on you, who you depend on. Details about individual people go to `areas/people/`, this is only the outline.

## How I decide

Are you fast or slow? Do you want options or a recommendation? What do you tend to postpone? Claude uses this to push back in the right places.

## Health

Only what changes advice: conditions, limits, habits you are working on. Records go to `areas/health/`.

## Long-term preferences and constraints

Things that are not up for debate: values, budgets you will not exceed, places you will not live.
