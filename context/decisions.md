# Decisions

> Append-only log. Never rewrite old entries.
> Record only decisions where you really weighed options.
> Ten entries a year is plenty.

Entry format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD · Short title

**Decision:** What you decided, in one or two sentences.

**Why:** The reasoning that mattered at the time.

**What I rejected:** The options you weighed and why they lost.

**Check later:** When and how you will know whether it was right.
```

---

## 2026-10-05 · Keep the second brain as plain Markdown on disk (example entry, keep or replace)

**Decision:** Notes are Markdown files in this repository, Obsidian is the interface, Git is the history, Claude is the AI layer.

**Why:** The tool should not own the knowledge. Plain text outlives any app, model or hosting service.

**What I rejected:** A hosted notes database (data with a third party, harder for agents to work with), several agents side by side (double cost for the same answer), a knowledge graph or RAG pipeline (solves a problem I do not have yet).

**Check later:** After three months: is the inbox really emptied weekly, and are the Bases views used on the phone?
